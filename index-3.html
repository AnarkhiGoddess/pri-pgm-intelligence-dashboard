U.S. Critical Minerals — Official 2025 List

All 60 commodities on the final USGS list. Each card links to image results for natural mineral, ore, or native-element specimens. Images must be captioned as ore/specimen where the pure element does not naturally occur.

Verified distinction: five PGMs — platinum, palladium, rhodium, ruthenium, and iridium — appear on the 2025 list. Osmium is a PGM but is not individually named on that list. This is official-list verification only; it does not verify production, reserves, import reliance, or a specific specimen identification.

Official source: USGS — About the 2025 List of Critical Minerals. Select “View natural-form images” to open Wikimedia Commons image search for each commodity.
